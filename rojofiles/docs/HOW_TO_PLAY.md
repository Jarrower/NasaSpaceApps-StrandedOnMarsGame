# How to play Mars Base 7

## Your mission

Your crew lives in a small outpost on Mars. Keep everyone alive for **7 sols** (a sol is a Mars day, about
24 hours and 39 minutes) and make **4 rocket fuel**. Then the rocket can fly you home!

You **lose** if Oxygen, Food or Health reaches **0**, or if there aren't enough sols left to make the fuel.

## Controls

| Action | Keyboard | Phone / tablet |
|---|---|---|
| Walk | W A S D | thumbstick |
| Jump | Space | jump button |
| Plug in / unplug a power cell | **E** at a Power Port | tap the prompt |
| Fix a leaking pipe | click the sparks, or **E** | tap |
| Next card | click **Next** | tap **Next** |

## One sol, step by step (about 1 minute)

1. **Read the banner.** It tells you if a dust storm is coming, or if it's quiet.
2. **Count your power cells.** You get **3** each sol (4 if your Solar Panels earned a bonus). They're shown at the
   bottom of the screen.
3. **Walk to a station's Power Port** (a metal post with ⚡) and press **E** to plug in a cell.
   Press **E** again to unplug it if you change your mind. Powered stations light up and glow.
4. **You can't power everything.** There are 5 stations and only 3 cells. Choose!
5. **Watch out for surprises** (see below) while the timer runs.
6. **When the timer ends,** every station changes a meter. The **Sol Report** shows what happened,
   for example *🌱 Greenhouse OFF → Food −30*.
7. A **"Did you know?"** card shows a real NASA fact. Press **Next**. All cells unplug and the next sol begins.

## The stations

| Station | If it has power | If it has no power |
|---|---|---|
| ☀️ **Solar Panels** | You get **+1 extra cell** next sol | No bonus |
| 🫁 **Oxygen Generator** | Oxygen **+10** | Oxygen **−40** |
| 🌱 **Greenhouse** | Food **+15** | Food **−30** |
| 🛡️ **Shield Bunker** | Health **+5**, and its door opens | Health **−10**, and its door is **locked** |
| ⛽ **Rocket Fuel Plant** | Rocket fuel **+1** | No fuel made |

Meters can't go above 100.

## Surprise events

Sol 1 is always calm. After that, at most one event happens each sol.

### 🌪️ Dust storm
You get a warning at the start of the sol, and the sky turns orange and dusty.
Dust blocks the Sun, so **Solar Panels can't give a bonus cell** this sol. Don't waste a cell on them!

### ☢️ Solar radiation storm
An alarm rings and you have **15 seconds** to get inside the **Shield Bunker**. A marker shows where it is.

| What happened | Result |
|---|---|
| Bunker has power and **everyone is inside** | Safe! No damage |
| Bunker has power but **someone is outside** | Health **−20** |
| Bunker has **no power** (door locked) | Health **−40** |

The bunker door always opens from the inside, so you can never get trapped.

### ☄️ Micrometeorite
A tiny space rock hits near a station and an air pipe starts sparking.
**Click the sparks (or press E) 5 times within 20 seconds.** If the crew doesn't fix it, Oxygen **−10**.

## Winning tips

- **Power the Solar Panels on calm sols.** One cell now gives you 4 cells next sol. Keep it going!
- **Skip the Solar Panels on dust storm sols** — they won't charge.
- **Look at your lowest meter.** One sol without the Oxygen Generator costs 40 Oxygen. Two in a row is dangerous.
- **Don't forget fuel.** You need 4 fuel in 7 sols, so you can only skip the Fuel Plant 3 times.
- **Keep the bunker powered when you can.** Solar storms come without warning.
- **Playing with friends?** Split up: one plugs in cells, another stays near the bunker.

## Winning and losing

- **Win:** survive all 7 sols with 4 fuel. The rocket launches with your crew and you see how often you powered each
  station.
- **Lose:** the screen tells you *what* ran out, *why*, and a tip for next time. Press **Try again**.

## New to the game?

When you first spawn, a short **camera tour** shows you each station. Press **Skip tutorial** if you already know
how to play, or **How to play** on the title screen to watch it again.
